Privacy Policy

Beacon Tactic · © 2026 Lei Liu

Beacon Tactic does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts to create, no email address to give, and nothing is sent to the developer.

No accounts No analytics No advertising No in-app purchases No crash reporting

1. Summary

The game runs entirely on your own computer. It has no sign-in, no user accounts, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or telemetry library, and no crash or error reporting. It contains no in-app purchases. Nothing you do in the game reaches the developer.

2. What is stored on your device

The game writes two things to your own user data folder. Both stay on your computer, are readable only by the game, and are never uploaded:

  • Settings — a small preferences file holding your UI language, window size, whether your microphone and speaker are enabled, any player names you have muted, and your saved crew loadouts.
  • Board snapshots — positions you saved yourself with the in-game save key (F8 on a keyboard), used to restore a match, plus the training position used by the practice walkthrough.

Deleting the game removes them. There is no other local storage.

3. Voice chat

If you turn on voice in a multiplayer match, the game captures your microphone only while you are holding the push-to-talk control, and sends those few seconds directly to the other people in that same match, over the connection the match already uses.

Voice audio is never recorded, never saved to disk, and never sent to the developer. It is not stored by the game or by any relay. When voice is off, the microphone is not used at all. Voice is disabled by default.

4. Network connections

The game only opens a network connection when you ask it to, by starting or joining a multiplayer match:

  • LAN play — direct connections to other players on your own local network, plus a brief local network broadcast (UDP port 7780) so nearby computers can see the room you are hosting. This never leaves your local network.
  • Online play — a WebSocket relay that you configure yourself. It defaults to 127.0.0.1:7800, meaning a relay you run on your own machine. The relay forwards game packets between players; the developer does not operate it and does not receive their contents.
  • Steam — the Steam edition may use Steam's own networking and voice services, which are governed by the Steam privacy policy and by your Steam settings. The Mac App Store edition contains no Steam integration at all.

As with any network connection, the server or peer you connect to necessarily sees your IP address. Single-player play opens no network connection at all.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It contains cartoon artillery violence with no blood, no nudity, no user-generated content, no advertising and no in-app purchases. Because it collects no data, it does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Third parties

No analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDK is included in the build. Single-player play contacts no third party. Multiplayer connects only to the peers or relay you selected yourself.

7. Your rights and control

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You have complete control: everything the game stores is a file in your own folder, and deleting the game, or deleting that folder, removes it permanently.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what the game transmits or stores, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date below will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy, or anything else about the game, can be raised as an issue on the project's public repository: github.com/chanliu1983/beacon-tactic-site/issues. Issues may be written in English or Chinese.

1. 摘要

游戏完全在你的电脑上运行。没有登录，没有用户账号，没有广告 SDK，没有统计或遥测库，也没有崩溃或错误上报。游戏不含内购。你在游戏里的任何操作都不会传到开发者那里。

2. 存储在本机的数据

游戏只会在你自己的用户数据目录里写两样东西。两者都留在你的电脑上，只有游戏能读取，永远不会上传：

  • 设置——一个小小的偏好文件，保存界面语言、窗口大小、麦克风和扬声器是否开启、你静音过的玩家昵称，以及保存的队伍配置。
  • 棋盘快照——你用游戏内存档按键（键盘上的 F8）亲自保存的局面，用于恢复对局，以及练习讲解用的训练局面。

删除游戏即一并删除。除此之外没有其他本地存储。

3. 语音聊天

如果你在多人对局中开启语音，游戏只在你按住按住说话按键期间采集麦克风，并把这几秒音频通过该对局已在使用的连接，直接发给同一局里的其他玩家。

语音音频绝不会被录制、不会被保存到磁盘、也绝不会被发给开发者。游戏和任何 relay 都不会存储它。语音默认关闭，关闭时麦克风完全不会被使用。

4. 网络连接

只有当你主动开始或加入多人对局时，游戏才会建立网络连接：

  • 局域网联机——与你本地网络内其他玩家的直连，以及一次很短的本地网络广播（UDP 7780 端口），让附近的电脑能发现你正在开的房间。这些流量不会离开你的局域网。
  • 在线联机——由你自己配置的 WebSocket relay，默认地址是 127.0.0.1:7800，也就是你自己机器上运行的 relay。relay 只在玩家之间转发对局数据包，开发者不运营它，也收不到其中的内容。
  • Steam——Steam 版可能使用 Steam 自己的网络与语音服务，其规则受 Steam 隐私政策和你的 Steam 设置约束。Mac App Store 版完全不包含 Steam 集成。

与任何网络连接一样，你所连接的服务器或对端必然会看到你的 IP 地址。单机模式完全不建立网络连接。

5. 儿童

本游戏适合全年龄。内容为卡通炮击，无血腥、无裸露、无用户生成内容、无广告、无内购。由于不收集任何数据，本游戏不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 第三方

构建版本中不包含任何统计、广告、归因或崩溃上报 SDK。单机模式不联系任何第三方。多人模式只连接你自己选择的玩家或 relay。

7. 你的权利与控制

因为不收集任何数据，开发者手上没有任何可供访问、更正、导出或删除的内容。完全的控制权在你：游戏存储的一切都是你自己目录下的文件，删除游戏或删除该目录即可彻底清除。

8. 政策变更

如果未来的版本改变了游戏的传输或存储内容，本页面会在该版本发布前更新，下方的生效日期也会随之改变。
